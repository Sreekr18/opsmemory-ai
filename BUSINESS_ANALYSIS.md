## 1. Problem Statement & Market Opportunity

In 2026, enterprise IT outages cost an estimated **$9,000 per minute** for Tier-1 digital businesses. Despite widespread adoption of observability platforms (Datadog, Dynatrace, New Relic) and incident management tools (PagerDuty, Incident.io, FireHydrant), post-incident knowledge remains largely unsearchable, fragmented in Slack threads and Google Docs.

When a recurring outage occurs (e.g., database connection pool exhaustion, Redis memory leak, Kubernetes pod eviction), on-call engineers spend an average of **45 to 90 minutes** re-investigating the exact same symptoms from scratch.
